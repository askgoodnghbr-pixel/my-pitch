Why I built this

While attending the 5Cs, I noticed that many students share the experience of dreading a trip to the dining hall. Common reasons include

  • Not wanting to socialize or be around other people at that moment
  • Long wait times just to get a meal
  • Distance, even when the food is appealing
  • Studying at the library and not wanting to pack up and leave
  • Feeling too sick to make the trip to the dining halls

The idea

I have experienced each of these myself, and it led me to ask a simple question - if a service existed that could bring a meal directly to me, would I actually pay for it? I believe many other students would answer yes. That question is the reason I am building Good Neighbor, a student to student delivery service that brings meals from dining halls to campus buildings. It also creates additional on-campus job opportunities for students who want to earn extra money.

Order

For students who want food
  • Pick your building and your food
  • Flat delivery fee
  • Dropped off in front of the building
  • Full refund if the order isn't completed

Deliver

For students who want to earn
  • Students who sign up get notified of new orders
  • They accept the ones they want
  • Pick up, drop off, and get paid
  • They get to walk or bike

What's next

The long-term goal is a community platform. Anyone could post a task they need done, and others could put money up for it to get it done. It starts with food and dining halls, which is the clearest need and the easiest place to get right. From there it can grow into a community helping itself.

Where we are

Good Neighbor is in limited beta. The site is live and collecting interest from students who want to order and students who want to deliver. I'd welcome your feedback and guidance as I shape this into something that works well for campus.
